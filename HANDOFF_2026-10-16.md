## 2. The plan — decided, do not re-ask

The owner said on 2026-10-16:
> «ملناش دعوة بالقديم، خلينا في اللي بنتفق عليه أنا وانت. عدّل كل الملفات بما يتوافق مع الخطة الحالية وأرشف اللي ملهوش لازمة.»

The quick questions:
- **Q1** (priority of outside-shop viewing) and **Q2** (the real category list): «تجاهلهم» (ignore them).
- **Q3**: «زي ما انت عملت» (as you built it), meaning a **PIN on every user switch**.

**Architecture:**
- The Windows desktop is the POS and the **single source of truth**, with one SQLite database. It sells with no internet.
- Android is a **companion** on the shop Wi-Fi:
  - **no database**, and no selling in V1;
  - QR pairing, every request authenticated and encrypted;
  - shows live sales (the owner sees everything; a seller sees only his own), price/stock lookup, camera scanner, photos.
- Cloud and remote viewing: phase 4, optional.

**Users:**
- PIN sign-in and quick switch, with a PIN on every switch.
- Owner approvals by owner PIN, replacing the Pattern.
- Seller discount limit: 5%.

**Selling:**
- **Payments:** cash, wallets, InstaPay, card (recorded only), instalment companies (recorded only), optional آجل (credit, needs a customer), and split payments. This replaces D3 (cash-only).
- **IMEI:** optional by default, with a "required" setting.
  - Quantity stays authoritative.
  - Receive by IMEI or by count; attach or register an IMEI at sale.
  - When sold without an IMEI, the oldest free unit is retired.
- **Warranty:** presets by product type (new 12 months, used 1 month) pre-fill the line dates and stay editable. This replaces D4 (no presets).
- **Returns:** partial or full, owner PIN, at the original line cost (D7). Damaged goods go to a damaged-stock bucket.
- **Report definitions** are decided (`PLAN.md` §12).

**Catalogue and costing:**
- Categories: a **2-level tree** managed by the owner.
- Product images: **optional**, stored as 192 px files and included in backups.
- Costing: `ADR-002` only — moving average for quantity items, actual cost per IMEI unit. FIFO and the method switch are retired.

**Backup:** manual, plus automatic at shift open/close and every 2 hours, a USB copy, rotation, and an integrity check. Restore needs a manual restart.

**Hardware:**
- No scanner today. Typed search (name, code, last 4 IMEI digits) and the companion's camera; a USB scanner can be added later.
- 80 mm thermal printer, Arabic printed as an image, plus PDF/WhatsApp.

**Roadmap:**
- Phase 0 is **done**. Phase 1 (a full selling day on the desktop) is next, then phases 2–4.
- A phase is accepted by **a real working day** with the drawer and stock at zero difference.

**Superseded:** `ADR-008`, `ADR-014`, `ADR-013` (decisions 1–3 and 9), `ADR-015` (§1–3 and the Pattern column), D3, D4.

**Kept from 2026-10-15:** D1 (optional supplier on receiving, implemented in `0014`), D5, D6, D7, D8/D9, and restore with a manual restart.
